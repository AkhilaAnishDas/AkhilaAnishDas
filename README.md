# `01` — ABOUT ME

I am **Akhila Anish Das**, a second-year **B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at ITM Skills University**, with a growing passion for software development, problem-solving, and technology.

I enjoy the process behind building things just as much as the final result.

For me, a programming problem is rarely just about writing code. It is about understanding the problem, experimenting with possible approaches, encountering errors, debugging patiently, and eventually finding a solution that works.

Currently, I work with **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, C++, React, and SQL**, while expanding my knowledge of **Java, DBMS, Node.js, and MongoDB**.

Beyond technology, I enjoy **Drawing, Writing, and Playing Badminton** — three completely different activities that keep my creative, analytical, and competitive sides equally active.

### A little more about how I think

```text
Problem
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Experiment
   ↓
Break something
   ↓
Debug
   ↓
Learn why it broke
   ↓
Build a better solution
   ↓
Repeat
```

> **Every error is another piece of the system I understand.**

# `05` — MY DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

### I follow a simple rule:

> **Understand first. Build second. Debug relentlessly. Improve continuously.**

I don't see debugging as a separate stage of programming.

**Debugging is programming.**

Every error teaches me something about:

* how the system works
* why my assumption was wrong
* how different components interact
* how to approach the problem differently next time

That mindset is one of the things I enjoy most about Computer Science.
